System Architecture & Trust

Built at the Edge of Your Business

Agent Factory answers at the point of contact — the phone, the SMS inbox — instead of routing a caller through a cloud contact centre. Here's how it's built, and what that means for the data your customers hand over.

1

Answers at the Edge

Twilio Voice and SMS webhooks hit the receptionist the moment a call or text arrives — no hold queue, no after-hours voicemail. The orchestrator answers, books, or escalates in the same conversation.

2

Org-Scoped by Design

Every contact, reminder, and consent record carries an org_id. Reads and writes are scoped to it at the database level, so one business's customer list cannot be queried from another's session.

3

Consent Before Contact

Nothing sends to a contact who hasn't opted in — silence is not treated as consent. A STOP reply is honoured instantly, from any state, with no confirmation step in between.

4

An Audit Trail You Can't Edit

Every consent change is appended to a permanent log — old state, new state, source, timestamp. There is no update or delete path for it, because an editable audit trail isn't evidence of anything.

Privacy & Consent

What This Means Under the Privacy Act

Federation University is based in Victoria, Australia — the platform's consent handling is built around the data-handling principles that matter most to a small business's customers.

🔒

Data Minimisation

A contact record holds only what a reminder needs: name, phone or email, channel preference, and quiet hours. No profiling fields, no data collected "just in case."

✅

Opt-In, Not Opt-Out

Under Australian anti-spam rules, the absence of a refusal is not consent. A contact must be explicitly opted in before the first reminder ever sends.

🌙

Quiet Hours, Respected

Every contact sets their own do-not-disturb window. A reminder due inside it is held, not sent — your customers' evenings stay theirs.

📜

A Record You Can Show

If a customer asks "did I agree to this?", the append-only consent log answers it — source, timestamp, and the state it changed to.

In plain terms: this describes how the consent and contact-isolation system is architected and tested, not a legal compliance certification. If you're piloting this for a regulated industry, review it against your own obligations before go-live.
