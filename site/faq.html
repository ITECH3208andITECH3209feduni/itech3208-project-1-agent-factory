Agent Factory is a multi-agent artificial intelligence platform developed as a capstone project for Federation University Australia (ITECH3208 / 3209). It integrates autonomous agents capable of conducting literature reviews across arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and PubMed, performing intelligent Amazon commercial product scraping and scoring, answering incoming voice and SMS phone calls via Twilio, and evaluating academic integrity.

Inbound phone calls are received via Twilio Webhooks at /twilio/voice. The system answers the caller using Amazon Polly (Joanna voice, Australian accent) and opens a Twilio Gather element to transcribe caller speech. The transcribed query routes through the Orchestrator to query the ChromaDB vector database, and the answer is streamed back via Polly TTS in multi-turn dialogue until the caller says goodbye.

When a caller or chat user requests an appointment, the system extracts the requested date, time, and topic. If Google Calendar service-account credentials are configured, the appointment is created directly in Google Calendar. If credentials are not present, it gracefully falls back to generating an RFC 5545 .ics calendar file downloadable directly from the dashboard.

The Academic Literature Review Agent queries three major academic sources simultaneously:
• arXiv: Preprints in Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, and Quantitative Biology.
• Semantic Scholar: Millions of peer-reviewed computer science, engineering, and interdisciplinary papers with citation counts.
• PubMed: The National Library of Medicine database for biomedical, health, and life science literature.

Yes. Every retrieved paper includes its DOI, author list, publication year, journal, and abstract. The Literature Agent provides a dedicated BibTeX exporter that automatically formats entries ready for inclusion into LaTeX .bib files.

When an outbound SMS or Voice call is initiated, a unique message record is registered in the SQLite delivery_history table. Twilio posts asynchronous delivery receipts to /twilio/status-callback with statuses such as delivered, undelivered, or failed along with carrier error codes. The dashboard updates live with these state transitions.

The /delivery/email/bounce endpoint receives standardized bounce webhook payloads from SMTP or email relay providers. Upon receiving a hard bounce or spam complaint, the delivery status is updated to bounced and the recipient address is permanently added to the email_suppressions table. Subsequent reminder attempts to that address are automatically blocked with a 400 Bad Request to protect domain sender score.

The Analytics engine computes:
• Delivery Rate %: Successfully delivered messages divided by total sent attempts.
• Response Rate %: Inbound reply interactions divided by outbound reminders.
• Channel Breakdown: Volume and success rates partitioned across SMS, Email, and Voice.
• Daily Volume Trends: 7-day time series tracking sent vs delivered vs failed counts.

Users can customize their theme (Dark, Light, High-Contrast) and select from 5 accent color palettes (Indigo, Emerald, Violet, Amber, Rose). Additionally, dashboard cards and widgets can be reordered or hidden. All preferences persist in both browser localStorage and via the /api/user/settings REST endpoint, with a one-click 'Reset to Default' button.
