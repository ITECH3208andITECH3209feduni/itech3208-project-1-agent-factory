What it does

Reminders, Consent and a Receptionist

How Agent Factory keeps customers informed without missing a call, sending a message they did not agree to, or doubling up.

⏰
Reminders

Reminders That Run Themselves

Schedule a reminder once, and it goes out on time by the channel each contact prefers, whether it is a single appointment or a weekly routine.

One-off and recurring

Pick a date and time, or repeat daily, weekly or on a custom interval. Times follow the organisation's timezone and stay correct through daylight saving.

Full control after scheduling

Edit, pause, resume, reschedule or cancel any reminder from the dashboard without recreating it.

Never sent twice

Each reminder is claimed once before sending, so a restart or a second worker cannot deliver it a second time.

🛡️
Consent & Privacy

Consent Is Checked Before Every Send

No message goes to a contact who has not opted in. The rule runs in the send path itself, not just in a form.

Opt-in required

A contact must be explicitly opted in before the first reminder. Silence is never treated as consent, and opted-out contacts are blocked.

STOP works automatically

STOP, START and HELP texts are handled before the AI sees them, and every change is written to an audit trail.

Quiet hours and preferences

Per-contact channel preference and quiet hours are respected, so nobody is messaged at 11pm.

📬
Delivery

SMS, Voice and Email From One Place

Reminders go out by the channel that suits the contact, with the status of every message recorded.

Three channels

Text message and voice calls through Twilio, and email through any standard SMTP provider. US text delivery depends on carrier registration, which is in progress.

Retries and bounces

Temporary failures retry with backoff. Hard email bounces go on a suppression list to protect the sending domain.

Delivery history

Twilio status callbacks record queued, sent, delivered and failed with the carrier error code, and the history can be exported as CSV.

📞
Communications

24/7 AI Receptionist

Answers calls and texts at any hour, books appointments, and hands over to a person when asked.

Answers, books, escalates

Common questions are answered from a knowledge base first. Booking uses Google Calendar with a calendar-file fallback and schedules a reminder automatically. Asking for a person flags the conversation for your team.

Keeps the booking in mind

A caller can ask about parking or what to bring in the middle of a booking, and the receptionist answers and then carries on with the booking.

Seven voice languages

Greets and speaks in English, Spanish, Hindi, Chinese, French and German, with the operator choosing the default.

📊
Operations & Analytics

Dashboard, Call Summaries and Reports

One screen shows what is coming up, what was sent, and which calls need attention first.

Live dashboard

Stats and activity update as they happen over a secured live connection, with delivery rate, response rate, channel breakdown and daily trends.

Call summaries

After each call the operator sees a one-line summary with a sentiment and a priority tag, so the urgent ones stand out.

Contacts and exports

Import contacts from CSV, manage them per organisation, and export reminder history and daily trends as CSV.

Also on the platform

Research Agents

The same platform also hosts three research agents from the first sprints, available in the web app alongside the receptionist.

Literature review

Searches arXiv, Semantic Scholar and PubMed in parallel, writes a short synthesis and exports BibTeX citations.

Amazon seller research

Scrapes and scores products and suppliers to help find and compare opportunities.

Academic integrity check

Estimates how likely a text is AI-generated and searches for matching sources.
