Deep Dive

Platform Capabilities

A comprehensive overview of the specialized agents, backend pipelines, and analytical tracking engines built into Agent Factory.

📚
Research Intelligence

Academic Literature Review Agent

Automates the literature search process across major scientific and medical repositories, delivering synthesized findings and exportable citations in seconds.

Multi-Repository Aggregation

Simultaneous parallel queries to Semantic Scholar (computer science/general), arXiv (physics, math, CS preprints), and PubMed (biomedical literature).

Claude AI Synthesis

Generates a coherent synthesis paragraph connecting key insights, methodological patterns, and consensus points across retrieved papers.

BibTeX Citation Export

One-click generation of formatted BibTeX citations ready for immediate inclusion in LaTeX and academic reference managers.

🛒
Commercial Intelligence

Amazon Product Intelligence & Scoring Engine

Extracts live product listings, customer sentiment signals, and pricing dynamics with automated anti-detection resilience.

Stealth Playwright Scraper

Headless Chromium automation configured with playwright-stealth to bypass aggressive anti-bot captchas and rate limits.

0–100 Quality Scoring

Algorithmic evaluation scoring products on review density, star rating calibration, price competitiveness, and prime badge verification.

RapidAPI Automatic Fallback

Fail-soft architecture that automatically falls back to secondary RapidAPI endpoints if direct scraping meets network anomalies.

📞
Communications

24/7 AI Receptionist with Twilio Integration

Operates across inbound SMS and voice calls, providing immediate answers, appointment bookings, and intelligent human escalation.

Polly.Joanna Voice Synthesis

Australian English voice synthesis with automated markdown stripping and phrase pacing optimized for phone conversations.

Google Calendar & iCal Booking

Direct appointment booking via Google Calendar service-account credentials with automatic fallback to standard `.ics` calendar files.

ChromaDB Vector Store RAG

Semantic search across crawled university pages and PDF documents to answer campus and organizational questions factually.

📊
Operations & Analytics

Delivery Status & Analytics Engine

Comprehensive tracking for all outbound messages, confirmations, callbacks, and bounces.

Twilio Status Callback Webhook

Real-time callback receiver logging message statuses (queued, sent, delivered, undelivered, failed) with carrier error codes.

Email Bounce & Suppression

Hard bounce detection that automatically adds bad addresses to a suppression list to protect domain reputation and prevent spam triggers.

Interactive KPI Gauges & Trends

Live delivery rate %, response rate %, channel breakdown donuts, and daily volume time-series charts on the operator dashboard.
