Built for Front Desks

Wherever a Missed Call Costs You a Booking

Three kinds of businesses where the phone rings while someone's hands are full — and every unanswered call is a customer who tries the next number.

🩺
Clinics

Appointment Reminders That Reduce No-Shows

Patients get an SMS or call confirming their slot and a reminder before it, while reception stays free to handle who's in front of them.

  • Automatic appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Google Calendar sync, with an .ics fallback
  • Quiet hours respected — no reminder at 11pm
💇
Salons

Bookings Taken Even Mid-Appointment

A stylist mid-cut can't pick up the phone. The receptionist can — it takes the booking, texts a confirmation, and reminds the client the day before.

  • Voice and SMS booking, Polly TTS in a natural voice
  • Per-client channel preference (SMS, email)
  • Opt-in consent, honoured from the first message
🔧
Trades

Never Lose a Job to Voicemail

A tradie on a roof or under a car can't answer. The receptionist takes the enquiry, captures the job, and keeps the follow-up reminder on track.

  • Inbound call and SMS handling, no missed-call voicemail
  • Delivery tracking so you know a reminder landed
  • Escalates to a human the moment it's asked to
Multi-Agent Ecosystem

One Platform, Three Specialised Agents

Reception and reminders lead — backed by the same platform's research synthesis and market intelligence agents.

📞
Communication & Ops

24/7 AI Receptionist

Natural conversation engine integrated with Twilio Voice and SMS, Amazon Polly TTS, multi-turn dialog memory, and automatic Google Calendar appointment booking.

  • Inbound SMS & phone call handling with Polly.Joanna voice
  • ChromaDB vector store RAG semantic search over site knowledge
  • Delivery tracking, email bounce handling, and operator dashboard
📚
Research Intelligence

Academic Literature Agent

Multi-source academic querying across arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and PubMed with automated Claude AI synthesis, deduplication, and BibTeX citation exports.

  • Unified query dispatch across 3 peer-reviewed repositories
  • Direct link generation and PDF download verification
  • Automated synthesis paragraph and BibTeX citation formatting
🛒
E-Commerce Scraper

Amazon Product Intelligence

Real-time e-commerce product search with dual-tier scraping, automated bot-detection avoidance via stealth Playwright, and a 0–100 quality scoring engine.

  • Playwright stealth scraping with RapidAPI secondary fallback
  • Multi-factor quality scoring (ratings, review velocity, price)
  • TTL disk caching to minimize latency and avoid rate limits
