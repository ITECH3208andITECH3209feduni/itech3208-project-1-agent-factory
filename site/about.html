Origin & Mission

About Agent Factory

Agent Factory was engineered as a capstone research and development project at Federation University Australia to explore real-world autonomous multi-agent systems.

Project Philosophy

Modern AI systems are often locked behind proprietary silos or designed solely as single-turn chat interfaces. Agent Factory tests an alternative paradigm: an agent factory where specialized, purpose-built autonomous agents collaborate across diverse modalities—from synthesizing scientific literature to answering live telephone inquiries and checking academic integrity.

Every agent is grounded in verifiable external knowledge: literature is backed by peer-reviewed DOI identifiers; receptionist queries are grounded in vector embeddings; and commercial intelligence uses multi-factor quality scoring to eliminate hallucination.

🛡️

Verifiability & Grounding

Strict grounding in real scientific repositories and crawled institutional documents. LLMs never invent citations or university policies.

⚡

Fail-Soft Engineering

Every critical external dependency—from Playwright scraping to Google Calendar booking—features an automatic local fallback (RapidAPI, iCal format, mock modes).

🔒

Privacy & Session Isolation

Session-scoped memory prevents cross-tenant data leakage. Outbound message recipients can be suppressed upon hard bounce to protect reputation.
